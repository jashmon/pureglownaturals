INGREDIENT CLARITY / EDUCATIONAL GUIDE

Read the label before you choose

A skincare label can look complex. Start with the job of the product. Is it a cleanser, moisturizer, serum, or sunscreen?

Read the directions and warnings. Check when and how to use it. Look at the full ingredient list, not one name on the front of the pack. One ingredient cannot tell you how the whole formula will work.

Words like "natural" and "clean" are broad. They do not prove that a product will suit your skin. Compare the details, price, and role of each product before you choose.

Add one new product at a time. Stop using it if irritation occurs. Ask a qualified health professional about skin problems that persist.
